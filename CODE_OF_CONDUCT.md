# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Tyravex is committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative open-source community.

We welcome contributors regardless of their experience, background, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Accept feedback and differing technical opinions.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations, not individuals.
- Help create an environment where new contributors can participate.
- Respect privacy and confidential information.
- Follow project contribution and security guidelines.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation
- Discriminatory or hateful language
- Personal attacks and repeated hostile behavior
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Spam or deliberate disruption of project discussions
- Other conduct that makes participation unsafe or unwelcome

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Tyravex repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project community spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and made in good faith.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers privately.

Until a dedicated community contact address is published, use an appropriate private GitHub contact channel rather than posting sensitive reports publicly.

Reports will be reviewed with consideration for the safety and privacy of everyone involved.
